## Gravel cycling

I ride a steel gravel bike, mostly on Saturday mornings on the back roads west of Austin. My longest ride was a 100-mile gravel event in the Texas Hill Country in 2023. I plan routes the night before and like riding alone.

## Cooking

I cook most evenings. My specialty is slow-smoked brisket, which takes about twelve hours, so it only happens when friends come over on a weekend.
