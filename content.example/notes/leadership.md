## Mentoring associate product managers

At Ledgerline I mentor three associate product managers. We meet every two weeks; they bring a decision they are stuck on, and we work through it together instead of me giving the answer. Two of them now lead their own discovery interviews. I have never managed product managers as direct reports.

## Leading two squads at Ledgerline

The integrations work at Ledgerline runs in two squads: one for ERP connectors and one for the public API, fourteen engineers and two designers in total. I run one shared planning session every six weeks and a short weekly sync with both tech leads, so the squads don't block each other.
